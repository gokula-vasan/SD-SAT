The Next Generation of Digital Banking & Money Transfer

A full-stack, enterprise-grade banking platform simulating real-time money transfers with MongoDB ACID multi-document transactions, idempotency protection, and immutable double-entry ledger bookkeeping.

🚀 Launch Banking Portal ✍️ Create Test Account
🔒

Secure Authentication

JWT-based stateless sessions, bcrypt password hashing with 10 salt rounds, input sanitization, and strict server-side role-based authorization.

🔄

ACID Multi-Document Transfers

Powered by MongoDB replica set sessions. Atomically debits sender and credits recipient with conditional balance verification to prevent race conditions.

🛡️

Idempotency Protection

Unique idempotency keys scoped to the user and operation prevent duplicate debits from network retries or double form submissions.

📒

Immutable Double-Entry Ledger

Every successful transaction creates matching debit and credit ledger entries stored in paise (integer minor units) for audit trail integrity.

👥

Beneficiary Management

Add and manage trusted recipient accounts with display aliases and quick 1-click transfer initiation.

🛠️

Admin Console & Audit Trail

Dedicated role-protected administrative dashboard for inspecting users, toggling account suspension, and reviewing comprehensive system audit logs.
